# Series circuits: study notes

In a series circuit the components are connected one after another, so there is
only one path for the current.

## Three rules

1. The same current flows through every component in the loop.
2. The voltages across the components add up to the supply voltage.
3. The total resistance is the sum of the individual resistances:
   R_total = R1 + R2 + R3 + ...

## Worked example

A 2 ohm resistor and a 3 ohm resistor are connected in series across a 10 V supply.

- Total resistance: 2 + 3 = 5 ohms.
- Current: I = V / R = 10 / 5 = 2 A. This same 2 A flows through both resistors.
- Voltage across the 2 ohm resistor: 2 A x 2 ohms = 4 V.
- Voltage across the 3 ohm resistor: 2 A x 3 ohms = 6 V.
- Check: 4 V + 6 V = 10 V, which matches the supply.

## Common mistake

Adding the currents of the two resistors. In a series circuit there is only one
current, and it is the same everywhere.
